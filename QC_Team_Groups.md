# QC Team Groups — E-763 Morava Corridor

Organizational grouping of the Quality Control team by discipline / work stream for the E-763 Morava Corridor highway project (Serbia). Each group owns inspection, testing oversight, and documentation for its scope. Standards referenced are indicative: **SRPS** (Serbian standards), **EN** Eurocodes, **PGBT** (Serbian road technical conditions), and project-specific Technical Specifications.

> Assign named engineers/technicians to each group as staffing is confirmed. Groups may share lab and documentation resources.

---

## 1. Earthworks & Geotechnical QC
**Scope:** Subgrade, embankments, cuttings, fill layers, capping, borrow pits and material sources.

**Key activities:**
- Layer-by-layer inspection (lot, chainage, layer ID) and release
- Field density (in-situ) and moisture control
- Bearing capacity / deformation checks (plate load Ev2, LWD/CBR)
- Material suitability and source approval

**Typical tests:** Proctor (max dry density / OMC), field density, plate load (Ev2, Ev2/Ev1 ratio), CBR, Atterberg limits, grading.

**Deliverables:** Layer approval records, compaction summaries, geotechnical NCRs.

---

## 2. Concrete & Structures QC
**Scope:** Cast-in-place and precast concrete, reinforcement, formwork, culverts, retaining walls, minor structures. *(Anchors the existing Concrete QC Dashboard.)*

**Key activities:**
- Pre-pour checks (rebar, cover, formwork, geometry)
- Fresh concrete control (slump, temperature, air, sampling)
- Cube/cylinder casting, curing and strength tracking by Plant & Mix
- Reinforcement certification and traceability

**Typical tests:** Compressive strength (7/28-day), slump/consistency, air content, temperature, concrete cover, rebar tensile certs.

**Deliverables:** Pour records, strength trend reports (dashboard), mix approval, concrete NCRs.

---

## 3. Asphalt & Pavement QC
**Scope:** Bituminous base, binder and wearing courses; pavement construction and surface quality.

**Key activities:**
- Mix design verification and plant output control
- Laydown temperature and compaction monitoring
- Core sampling for thickness, density and air voids
- Surface regularity and skid resistance

**Typical tests:** Marshall / mix composition, binder content and grading (extraction), in-situ density (cores/nuclear), air voids, thickness, evenness (IRI/straightedge), texture depth.

**Deliverables:** Asphalt lot records, core test reports, pavement NCRs.

---

## 4. Drainage & Utilities QC
**Scope:** Culverts, pipe drainage, ditches, manholes, subsurface drainage and service crossings.

**Key activities:**
- Trench, bedding and backfill inspection
- Pipe laying alignment, gradient and jointing checks
- Watertightness / flow verification
- Backfill compaction around structures

**Typical tests:** Bedding grading, backfill compaction, pipe gradient survey, leakage/watertightness tests.

**Deliverables:** Drainage installation records, as-built gradient checks, drainage NCRs.

---

## 5. Bridges & Major Structures QC
**Scope:** Bridges, viaducts, overpasses/underpasses, large-span and heavily-reinforced/prestressed structures.

**Key activities:**
- Foundations, piers, abutments, bearings and expansion joints
- Prestressing / post-tensioning oversight and grouting
- Structural concrete and high-grade reinforcement control
- Girder erection, deck and geometry survey

**Typical tests:** High-strength concrete cubes, prestress force/elongation records, bearing and joint installation checks, survey/geometry verification, weld/NDT where steel is involved.

**Deliverables:** Structure inspection records, PT logs, survey reports, structural NCRs.

---

## 6. Materials & Laboratory QC
**Scope:** Central/site lab, incoming material certification, calibration, cross-disciplinary testing support.

**Key activities:**
- Material approval and certificate registry (cement, aggregate, bitumen, steel, additives)
- Test scheduling and frequency compliance
- Equipment calibration and lab accreditation upkeep
- Sample custody and result reporting to discipline groups

**Typical tests:** Aggregate grading/soundness/LA abrasion, cement and bitumen properties, water quality, plus support testing for Groups 1–5.

**Deliverables:** Material approval register, calibration log, consolidated test database.

---

## 7. Documentation, NCR & Compliance
**Scope:** QC records management, non-conformance workflow, audit and standards compliance across all groups.

**Key activities:**
- Maintain ITPs (Inspection & Test Plans) and checklists
- Log, track and close NCRs; corrective/preventive actions
- Test-frequency and compliance tracking against Technical Specifications
- Interface with client/supervision and audit readiness

**Typical outputs:** ITP register, NCR log & status, compliance/frequency matrices, monthly QC reports.

**Deliverables:** Document control register, NCR dashboard, audit trail.
